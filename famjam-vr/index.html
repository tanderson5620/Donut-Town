QUEST: point at the screen and pull the trigger for the menus. In the game: left stick moves, A shoots (hold, let go at the top) or jumps to block on defense, B passes or goes for the steal, right trigger shoves (or dunks with the ball), grip turbo, X changes your seat (upper deck, courtside, tabletop), Y pauses.
VOICE CHAT: online, turn on VOICE in the lobby or the pause menu to talk with everyone in the game (tap MIC FOR VOICE CHAT first, here, so the headset lets the game use its microphone).
KEYBOARD: the same keys as the arcade game (arrows or WASD, J shoot, K pass, Shift turbo, Esc pause), V changes your seat, drag with the right mouse button to look around.
